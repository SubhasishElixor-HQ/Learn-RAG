# The Lazy Student

I opened my book at half past ten,
Then closed it quickly—“I'll read again!”
My laptop said, “Let's start the grind,”
I said, “Tomorrow, you'll change my mind.”

The teacher asked, “Where is your work?”
I smiled politely and tried to lurk.
“My assignment is almost done,”
It exists somewhere… just not on my laptop, son!

I studied one page, then took a break,
A five-minute rest became a sleep marathon by mistake.
The exam came fast, my brain said, “Bye!”
I wrote my name—and felt I should fly!

So remember, friends, when exams appear,
Don't wait until the deadline is near.
Study a little, don't be lazy,
Or your result might drive you crazy! 😂
